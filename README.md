# foodbridge-app
A smart food donation platform connecting donors with NGOs.

## 🌟 Overview

FoodBridge is a web-based food donation 
platform that connects food donors with 
nearby NGOs in real-time. Our mission is 
to reduce food waste and ensure that 
surplus food reaches those who need it most.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 😔 Problem
- India wastes tones of food every year.
- Millions of people go hungry daily
- No proper platform exists to connect common donors with NGOs quickly

 ✅ Our Solution
FoodBridge provides a **three-way system**
connecting Donors, NGOs, and Admins on 
one platform with real-time features.

✨ Features

 👤 For Donors
- 📝 Post surplus food donations
- 📍 Share live location for pickup
- 📊 Track donation impact
- 🆘 Food SOS — emergency NGO alert
- ⚠️ Expiry alerts for food items
- 📈 Personal impact dashboard

 🏢 For NGOs
- 🔔 Real-time donation notifications
- ✅ Accept/decline donations
- 📅 Pickup schedule management
- 🆘 Emergency food request (NGO SOS)
- 📍 Live donor location tracking
- 📊 Performance analytics

 🔐 For Admins
- 👥 User management
- 📊 Platform analytics & charts
- 🚨 Complaints management
- ✅ NGO verification
- 📈 Impact reports

 🌟 Unique Features
- 🆘 **Food SOS** — One button emergency
  alert to nearby donors/NGOs
- ⏰ **Expiry Alerts** — Auto notify 
  before food expires
- 📍 **Live Location** — Real-time 
  donor tracking on map
- 🌐 **Bilingual** — Hindi & English
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** — Full dark theme
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** — Works on 
  all screen sizes
